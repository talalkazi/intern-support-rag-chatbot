Working Hours & Daily Expectations:
Interns are expected to complete 15-20 hours per week asynchronously. While there are no fixed daily shift hours, interns typically allocate 3 to 4 hours per working day.

Task Submissions & Formats:
To submit your completed task assignment, log into the Intern Portal, navigate to the Dashboard, select 'Task Submission', upload your GitHub project link or code repository, and click 'Submit Assignment'.
Supported submission formats include GitHub repository URLs, PDF documentation, ZIP files for code archives, and Jupyter Notebook (.ipynb) files.

Task Deadlines, Extensions & Late Submissions:
If you miss a task deadline, submit a request via the portal ticket system for an extension within 24 hours of the missed deadline.
Assignments can be submitted after the official deadline has passed only if an extension request has been approved through the portal ticket system.

Technical Support & Contact:
If you experience technical issues with your assigned work, portal access, or development tools, reach out to the technical support team by opening a ticket under the 'Technical Helpdesk' section on the portal or message your assigned mentor on the official Slack/Discord workspace.

Portal Access & Credentials Reset:
If you get locked out of your account or forget your credentials, click on the 'Forgot Password' link on the portal login page or submit a credential recovery request under 'Portal Access' to reset your password.

Intern ID & Profile Information:
You can locate your Intern ID, active project status, and personal details by clicking on your avatar/name in the top-right corner of the Intern Portal and selecting 'Profile Settings'.
